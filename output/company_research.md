## Company Overview
Komodo Health is a healthcare analytics company that aims to reduce the global burden of disease by providing smarter use of data. The company was founded in 2014 and is headquartered in New York, NY. Komodo Health has raised over $314 million in funding from investors such as Oak HC/FT, ICONIQ Growth, and Andreessen Horowitz.

## Mission and Values
Komodo Health's mission is to reduce the global burden of disease. The company's values include being awesome, seeking growth, delivering "wow," and enjoying the ride. Komodo Health believes in being present, respectful, and accountable, and in fostering trust and community. The company also values learning, innovation, and bold execution.

## Recent News or Changes
In June, Komodo Health launched MapLab Enterprise, its AI-native healthcare analytics platform for organizations seeking to bring consistency to their data foundation. The company has also been expanding its team, with recent hires including a Chief Technology Officer and a Chief Product Officer.

## Role Context and Product Involvement
The Applied AI Engineer role at Komodo Health is part of the company's AI team, Labs@Komodo. The team is responsible for delivering AI-native solutions that elevate both the products Komodo Health brings to market and the way the company builds them. The Applied AI Engineer will work on defining strategic AI capabilities, setting technical standards, and acting as a cross-functional multiplier.
